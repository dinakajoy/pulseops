# Business Goals

## Problem Statement
Engineering teams often coordinate production incidents using disconnected tools such as Slack, emails, spreadsheets, and ticketing systems. This fragmented approach makes it difficult to coordinate responses, maintain a reliable incident history, measure operational performance, and continuously improve incident response.

## Goal
Provide engineering organizations with a centralized operational workspace that improves the coordination, visibility, and efficiency of production incident management.

## Objectives
- Standardize the incident response process across engineering teams.
- Reduce the time required to detect and resolve production incidents.
- Improve collaboration between Support, Engineering, and Management.
- Increase visibility into incident status and operational performance.
- Preserve a complete and auditable history of incident activities.
- Continuously improve operational efficiency through measurable metrics.

## Success Metrics
### Business KPIs
| Metric                      | Target               |
| --------------------------- | -------------------- |
| Mean Time to Detect (MTTD)  | Decreasing over time |
| Mean Time to Resolve (MTTR) | Decreasing over time |
| Critical incident frequency | Decreasing over time |
| Incident reopening rate     | < 5%                 |
| Internal user satisfaction  | > 4.5 / 5            |

### Operational KPIs
| Metric                        | Target      |
| ----------------------------- | ----------- |
| Incident creation time        | < 2 minutes |
| Incident assignment time      | < 5 minutes |
| Timeline completeness         | >95%        |
| Notification delivery success | >99.9%      |

## Expected Business Outcomes
- Faster coordination during production incidents.
- Improved visibility into operational health.
- Consistent incident management practices across teams.
- Better collaboration between Support, Engineering, and Management.
- Increased confidence in historical operational data for reporting and continuous improvement.
