# Product Context

## Product
PulseOps - An Internal Engineering Operations Platform for Multi-Tenant SaaS Companies

## The Business
A company operates a multi-tenant SaaS platform serving hundreds of client organizations. These organizations depend on the platform to run critical business operations. As the platform grows, maintaining service reliability becomes increasingly complex. When production issues occur, engineering teams need a structured way to detect, coordinate, investigate, and resolve incidents efficiently.

## Problem Statement
As a multi-tenant SaaS platform grows in customers, services, and engineering teams, production incidents become more frequent, more complex, and more difficult to coordinate.
Without a dedicated engineering operations platform, incident response is often coordinated through Slack, emails, spreadsheets, and ad hoc meetings. Information becomes fragmented, ownership is unclear, communication is inconsistent, and valuable operational knowledge is lost over time. 
Common challenges include:
- No centralized incident tracking
- Confusion over ownership
- Delayed communication
- Lack of visibility for management
- Inconsistent incident resolution
- Poor historical reporting
- Missing audit trails

## Why Now?
Modern SaaS platforms are becoming increasingly distributed, serving many client organizations across multiple services, integrations, and infrastructure components. As engineering teams grow and systems become more complex, operational incidents require structured coordination rather than ad hoc communication. Organizations need a dedicated platform that provides visibility, accountability, and standardized incident management practices.

## Target Customers
PulseOps targets organizations that develop and operate software products requiring coordinated incident management across engineering, support, and operations teams.
Examples include:
- SaaS companies
- FinTech companies
- Healthcare platforms
- E-commerce companies
- Logistics companies
- EdTech companies
- Government technology teams

## Product Summary
PulseOps is an internal engineering operations platform that enables organizations to detect, coordinate, track, and resolve production incidents affecting their products and services.

The platform provides a centralized workspace where support, engineering, and management teams can manage incidents from creation to resolution, assign responders, communicate progress, maintain a complete audit trail, and analyze operational performance over time.
